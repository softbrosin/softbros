Privacy Policy

Effective date: 13-11-2025

This Privacy Policy applies to the Softbros IN & Cluvana multi-instance B2B platform.

1. Introduction

Welcome — Softbros IN ("we", "us", "our") builds high-concurrency B2B software and club operations platforms hosting separate database schemas for each client business. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use and protect it, and the choices available to users. Each customer instance has isolated data and configuration schemas.

2. Summary

  • We host separate instances per business; member and financial data are strictly isolated per schema.
  • The platform supports biometric authentication (Face / Fingerprint) where available; we do not store biometric data.
  • Users authenticate via designated credentials or Tenant Subdomains to access authorized operations.

3. Data We Collect

3.1 Data you provide

  • Account Identifiers: Membership number, phone number, card ID, staff credentials.
  • Transactional Records: Dining orders, room bookings, court slots, payment status.

3.2 Technical & telemetry data

  • Device identifiers, browser version, and operating system.
  • Push notification tokens (Firebase Cloud Messaging).

4. Data Segregation & Multi-Tenancy

Each club or commercial client operates in an isolated PostgreSQL database schema. No member ledger, dining check, or payroll record is ever accessible by another organization.

5. Security Standards

All communications are encrypted using HTTPS/TLS. We utilize role-based access control (RBAC), optional SMS OTP (Ping4SMS), and Authenticator app (TOTP) two-factor verification.
