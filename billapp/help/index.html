  1. License Exceeded – What To Do Next

    Your license has exceeded its validity period, which means your subscription has expired. To continue using the service, you will need to renew your license. Please contact the vendor for renewal.

    On the login page, you will find a blue button. Click on it to access the vendor's contact number or email, along with your license ID. Kindly share this information with your vendor for further assistance.
