Tailored Role Workflows

Designed for Every Person on the Club Floor

From committee leaders and kitchen chefs to waiters and members, Cluvana eliminates manual friction across all operational touchpoints.

Effortless Privileges & Table Service

Members experience effortless luxury with mobile ordering, instant waiter buzzers, and complete transparency into their dining bills and annual dues.

  • ✓
    Table-Side Ordering & Buzzer: Browse food & beverage menus and tap "Call for Service" to sound an audio chime for their waiter.
  • ✓
    Live Bill Balance & Dues: View unpaid dining/bar orders, annual subscription status, and penalties directly on their profile.
  • ✓
    Sports & Cottages Booking: Reserve guest rooms, tennis courts, and swimming pool slots with instant confirmation.

Mobile Speed on the Floor

Waiters take table-side orders on smartphones and tablets, eliminating lost paper slips and unnecessary trips to the kitchen line.

  • ✓
    Table-Side Punching: Punch orders by table number; food instantly routes to the kitchen screen, drinks route to the bar station.
  • ✓
    Real-Time Table Buzzer Alerts: Audio and visual screen notifications trigger the moment a seated member requests service.
  • ✓
    Ticket Modifications: Add extra rotis or cancel an item without walking back to a fixed POS terminal.

Zero Lost Slips & Organized KDS

Kitchen chefs see a clear digital queue of active tickets organized by station (Veg, Non-Veg, Bar) with live elapsed timers.

  • ✓
    Live Kitchen Monitor: Orders pop up instantaneously via WebSockets with clear station tags and modification notes.
  • ✓
    Ready-to-Serve Notifications: One tap marks food as "Ready", instantly chiming the assigned floor waiter for pickup.
  • ✓
    Peak Rush Efficiency: Eliminates dropped, grease-stained, or misplaced handwritten paper KOTs.

Fast POS & Instant Thermal Receipts

Cashiers generate bills in seconds, calculate statutory food and liquor taxes automatically, and print directly to thermal receipt printers.

  • ✓
    1-Click Bill Generation: Select table, room, or member number to generate final tax invoices with automated GST & VAT.
  • ✓
    Multi-Mode Settlements: Accept UPI QR, Cash, Credit Card, Cheque, or mark the bill as pending balance on member ledger.
  • ✓
    Direct ESC/POS Thermal Printing: Driver-free printing to 58mm/80mm USB and Bluetooth printers with cash drawer kick.

Front Desk Member & Room Allocation

Receptionists verify membership status instantly, allocate guest cottages, record companion tariffs, and assign sports court passes.

  • ✓
    Instant Verification: Search members by phone number or ID to see active standing and privileges.
  • ✓
    Guest Rooms Management: View live room availability (Clean, Occupied, Reserved) and link room tariffs to the host member.
  • ✓
    Facility Passes: Issue guest court passes for tennis, badminton, and swimming pool slots without manual registers.

Executive Control, Audits & Payroll

General Managers, Secretaries, and Treasurers have complete control over staff payroll compliance, liquor audits, and financial statements.

  • ✓
    Indian Statutory Payroll: Computes monthly staff salaries with PF, ESI, Professional Tax, TDS, and salary advance repayments.
  • ✓
    Liquor Shrinkage Audits: Track bar inventory down to the milliliter and peg; review breakage and wastage logs with audit trails.
  • ✓
    Annual Subscriptions: Trigger batch membership renewal billing and monitor dues collection across the club calendar.
